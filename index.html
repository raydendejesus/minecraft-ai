How correcting works

Dolphin got it wrong (wrong size, block, door side…)? Just tell it in chat — it undoes and rebuilds.
Want detail? Say "make it detailed", or attach a 📷 photo of a build.
Builder got it wrong (the commands are bad)? Edit the commands, hit Fix & rebuild. That's saved as a lesson.
Right now? Hit 👍 so Dolphin remembers what you meant.
Then Retrain so the Builder actually learns your fixes.
